Three commands to your first gong

Runs on Cloudflare Workers (free tier is plenty), Node, Docker, Bun or Deno. Zero runtime dependencies.

  1. Install and set your Stripe signing secret

    npm i && npx wrangler secret put STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET

  2. Add your Discord and/or Slack webhook

    npx wrangler secret put DISCORD_WEBHOOK_URL   # and/or SLACK_WEBHOOK_URL

  3. Deploy

    npx wrangler deploy

Then in Stripe add a webhook endpoint at https://<your-host>/webhook for checkout.session.completed, checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded and invoice.paid. Try it without Stripe: npm run simulate 49. The full guide, including Docker and the browser gong, is in the README.

A menu bar app for your Mac

A native SwiftUI menu bar app with no third-party dependencies. It shows your numbers at a glance and rings when a sale lands.

  • MRR and today's change, with a 14 day sparkline
  • Day streak, labelled in days up
  • Goal progress, labelled in percent left
  • Sales today and this month
  • A live list of recent sales

Every number carries its unit, and the numbers are never faked. It talks to your own server over /mrr and /events.
